Inbox Dashboard: Privacy Policy

Inbox Dashboard is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jessica Westmont. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads her Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Tasks. It creates Gmail drafts, calendar events and tasks only when she asks. It never sends email.

Where data goes

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising. Summaries may be generated with AI services acting on the owner's behalf.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
